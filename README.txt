NEHA & LAILA — BIRTHDAY WEBSITE

1) Keep index.html and the assets folder together.
2) To preview on a computer, open index.html in Chrome.
3) To share as a website link, upload the whole folder to Netlify Drop:
   https://app.netlify.com/drop
   (You may need to sign in; upload the unzipped folder.)
4) On phone, Neha can scroll down naturally through the photos and messages.
5) Music starts only after she taps "Play our song" because mobile browsers block autoplay.
6) The video is included in assets/our-special-memory.mp4. It has its own play controls.
